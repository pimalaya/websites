# Bring www to the state of Pimalaya as of 2026-10-13

Catalogue re-grounded against GitHub and crates.io on 2026-10-13. neverest (0.3.0) now syncs mail, contacts and calendars into a pimdir store and moved to the Email + Contacts + Calendar domain; calendula (0.2.0) lists CalDAV, Google Calendar, vdir and pimdir; cardamum (0.3.0) gained vdir; himalaya (2.2.1) lists m2dir and mbox; io-msgraph covers calendars; io-pim-discovery discovers JMAP; io-pimdir became Library + CLI. New rows: io-mbox, io-vdir, and the pimdir specification (kind Specification, typed TechArticle in the JSON-LD). pimalaya-android reads closed alpha, in its row and its experiments panel. The home page's crate chips gained ical and io-pimdir.

The roadmap follows the 2026 to 2027 NLnet direction: standardise pimdir (now, stressed by the Android app), consolidate the libraries, interfaces as views over the local store, then tasks.

The sign-in page, its footer link, the business page row, the home note and the offer data were removed: the paid one-step sign-in plan was abandoned on 2026-10-06.

Capabilities moved: **www-site** (Five pages, Two audiences on the home page, Business page, Site-local pieces, Head and structured data; Sign-in page removed).
