# Refresh the catalogue against the releases, sort by name, tighten /business/

Statuses were re-grounded against GitHub releases and crates.io on 2026-09-29. calendula (0.1.0), tcard and tcal (0.2.0) moved from in development to early and joined the home grid, which gained a Calendar domain. Himalaya joined the grid too, next to its flagship section. mirador became carillon (renamed, 0.1.0, early); io-people became io-gpeople. New libraries: ical-rs, io-gcal, io-managesieve, io-pimdir, io-proxy, io-sasl; vcard shows as its crate name vcard-rs. io-replica, folded into io-pimdir, moved to the retired table. The roadmap now names io-pimdir as the store and sync engine under neverest.

Ecosystem rows are sorted by name instead of status then a hand-kept popularity rank; the `popularity` field and the unused `retiring` status were deleted.

The business page and the shared offer copy were tightened: shorter pains, one-line deadlines, one-line benefits, a single "what we bring" paragraph carrying the efficiency argument, and terser limits, partners and contact sections.

Capabilities moved: **www-site** (Products catalogue).
