# Delta

## ADDED Requirements

- www-site: AI stance. The business page and the community page SHALL state that Pimalaya ships no AI and that its tools are made to be driven by other software, agents included (JSON output, JSON schemas, documentation). They SHALL name an integration (such as MCP) as available only once it exists.

## MODIFIED Requirements

- www-site: Business page. Sign-in and discovery are part of the libraries' correctness; the integrator offer adds audit material instead (a bill of materials and the security policy of each supported release). Each offer SHALL carry a starting yearly price and MAY carry a setup fee shown under it. The provider offer SHALL include apps published and maintained under the provider's name, with a note on which platforms are available today. The pains SHALL include the yearly cost of keeping an app alive. The integrator offer SHALL NOT promise to verify or audit a company's own OAuth apps.

## REMOVED Requirements

None.
